Selected work

Three pieces of the retail platform I’ve shipped at IBEE Hosting, and what each one was for.

Moving a retail POS from native Android to Flutter

IBEE Hosting · 2025

FlutterDartHiveSpring BootMongoDB
The problem
The point‑of‑sale app stores ran on was a legacy native Android codebase. Every new store meant more manual setup, and any outage at the counter meant lost sales.
What I built
Rebuilt the app in Flutter, with ordering designed offline‑first: Hive holds orders locally and the app keeps taking them when connectivity drops, syncing back to Java and Spring Boot services on MongoDB once it returns.

Result: deployed and scaling across 50+ retail stores, with POS operations continuing through connectivity loss.

An integration layer for six third‑party systems

IBEE Hosting · 2025

QuarkusJavaRedisRabbitMQRESTWebhooks
The problem
The platform needed to talk to six external services, each with its own APIs and callbacks, without slowing down the store or blocking on slow partners.
What I built
60+ Quarkus REST and webhook endpoints, with Redis caching the hot paths and RabbitMQ moving slow or retry‑prone work off the request cycle so a partner outage never stalls a sale.

Result: six integrations live on one consistent, asynchronous backbone.

A print service that doesn’t lose receipts

IBEE Hosting · 2025

WebSocketsDurable queuesCross‑platform
The problem
Receipt and kitchen printing is the step customers notice when it fails, and printers drop off the network constantly in real stores.
What I built
A cross‑platform print service: the POS pushes jobs over WebSockets into durable queues, so a job survives a printer or app restart and prints when the device comes back instead of vanishing.

Result: more reliable printing and clean recovery across store operations.
